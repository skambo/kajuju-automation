## Meal Packages (add-ons to any room rate)

- **Bed & Breakfast (B&B):** included in Twin Garden and Deluxe room rates. Covers daily breakfast
  and a tea tray. The complimentary barista coffee/day is a Workation package perk only; it is not
  part of the standard B&B rate.
- **Half Board:** +2,500 KES/person/day — breakfast plus one main meal (lunch or dinner).
  Beverages beyond the included coffee/tea are extra.
- **Full Board:** +3,300 KES/person/day — breakfast, lunch, and dinner (starter, main, dessert per
  meal). Beverages extra.
- **Self-Catering:** kitchen access, available in the Penthouse Loft and Cottage.

## Booking Flow

1. Guest submits a request with dates, room preference, guest count, and meal preference.
2. Lodge confirms availability and holds the dates for 24 hours.
3. An invoice with M-Pesa payment details is sent.
4. A 50% deposit secures the booking; the remaining balance is due at arrival.

Required booking form fields: full name, phone number, email address, preferred room, number of
guests, dates, meal preference.

## Ambiguities & Gaps Found During Crawl

The assistant must **never guess** on these — it should say it isn't certain and offer to check
with the team, rather than invent a confident answer:

1. **Cancellation window 48–72 hours before arrival** — the source material states "full refund
   72+ hours before" and "no refund within 48 hours" but never states what happens in the 48–72
   hour window. Treat as unspecified; the assistant should say it will confirm rather than assume
   either full or no refund.
2. **Rate discrepancy between the general rate card and the workation package page** — e.g. the
   Deluxe Room weekday single rate reads differently on the two source pages (10,500 vs 12,500).
   `knowledge.json` records the workation-specific rate under the workation package and the
   standard rate under the room listing; the assistant should quote the rate that matches the
   guest's stated use case (workation vs. standard stay) and flag if genuinely ambiguous.
3. **Maximum group size for "larger groups on inquiry"** — no upper bound given; the assistant
   should say larger groups are handled case-by-case rather than name a number.
4. **Swimming pool / gym / spa on-site** — source material says there is no pool yet but one was
   under construction at time of crawl, with no completion date given. Treat "is there a pool" as
   answerable only with "not yet, no completion date available" — never invent a date.
5. **Exact festive-period date range** — "festive period" (3-night minimum for buyout, 2-night
   minimum for holidays) is referenced but its calendar boundaries are never defined on the site.
6. **Late checkout / early check-in outside the workation package** — described as "subject to
   availability" with no fixed fee (except the workation-specific 1,500 KES early check-in) — the
   assistant should not quote a fee for late checkout on a standard stay.
