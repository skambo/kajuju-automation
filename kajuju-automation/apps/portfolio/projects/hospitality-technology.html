Hospitality Technology & Digital Operations

The Operational Challenge

Running a hospitality business involves coordinating reservations, payments, guest services and daily operations across multiple systems.

I work with these systems firsthand and understand how disconnected workflows, unreliable integrations and manual processes can affect both operational efficiency and the guest experience.

These are third-party platforms that I select, configure and use as an operator. I did not build them. Using them under real conditions shows where technology adoption succeeds or breaks down: when availability falls out of sync between channels, when a payment step confuses a guest, or when a workflow is too brittle for a busy team.

Where Technology Meets Operations

  • Reservations and booking flows guests use directly
  • Channel management and OTA distribution, keeping availability consistent
  • Payment workflows from the guest's and the operator's side
  • Guest communication, including enquiries arriving through WhatsApp

Supporting Technical Evidence

Where I do build, it is to protect those operations. I designed and built a Playwright and TypeScript automation framework, with CI-driven double-booking detection and synthetic monitoring, to verify the booking, availability and guest-communication systems end to end.
