Interactive Digital Learning Platform

The Challenge

The challenge was to translate a social issue into an accessible, interactive digital learning experience that young people could engage with and learn from.

The platform needed to turn programme objectives and stakeholder requirements into practical, scenario-based learning experiences while remaining accessible, responsive and straightforward to use.

Approach & Delivery

Understanding the requirements

Worked with programme stakeholders to understand the intended audience, learning objectives and functional requirements, translating these into a practical technical approach.

Prototyping the experience

Created an early proof of concept to demonstrate the proposed scenario-based learning experience and help communicate how the solution could work before full implementation.

Development & quality

Built the interactive platform using Next.js, React and TypeScript, with responsive design, accessibility considerations, automated testing and privacy-conscious analytics incorporated into development.

Deployment & handover

Deployed the completed platform and prepared technical documentation and handover materials to support its continued use and future development.

Outcome

Delivered a functional, accessible digital learning platform that translates a complex programme challenge into an interactive learning experience.

Technology

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Playwright, Vercel.
