About

My career began in software quality engineering, working across fintech, design software and digital platforms. Over the years, I've worked closely with developers, designers and product teams to improve user experiences, strengthen software quality and support the delivery of complex products.

My work has increasingly expanded into technical delivery. I've worked directly with stakeholders to understand programme objectives, translate business requirements into technical solutions, develop interactive digital products, validate their quality and support implementation and handover.

Alongside my technology career, I own and operate a hospitality business in Kenya. This has given me a first-hand understanding of the challenges businesses face when adopting technology, from reservations and payments to operational workflows and guest experience.

These experiences shape how I approach technology: understanding the problem first, building practical solutions and reliable software, and ensuring the end product works for the people who use it.
