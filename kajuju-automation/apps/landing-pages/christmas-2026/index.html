Good to know

  • Minimum stay: Two nights between 19 December 2026 and 3 January 2027. Three nights for full property buyouts.
  • Deposit: A 50% deposit is required to secure your reservation. Payment should be completed within 48 hours of receiving booking instructions. The remaining balance is payable on arrival.
  • Cancellation: Cancel at least 72 hours before your arrival date for a full refund. Cancellations made less than 72 hours before arrival, no shows and early departures are not refundable.
  • Children: Children aged 11 and under can share existing bedding with their parents at no additional accommodation charge. Please contact us if a separate bed or cot is needed. Guests aged 12 and above are charged adult rates. Children aged 4 to 11 pay half the adult meal rates. Younger children can order from the café children's menu.
